Privacy Policy

Business Name: ARCJAPAN | Last Updated: 2026-05-28

1. Overview

ARCJAPAN operates as a sole-proprietor seller on Amazon.co.jp. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, protect, and delete customer personal data obtained through the Amazon Selling Partner API solely for order fulfillment.

2. Data Collected

We collect: buyer name, shipping address, phone number, and order ID from Amazon SP-API, solely for shipping label generation and order fulfillment. We do NOT collect payment information or email addresses.

3. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored in an encrypted database (AES-256 full-disk encryption). API credentials are stored with owner-only file permissions (chmod 600). Logs mask PII fields with regex filters. Logs retained for minimum 12 months.

4. Data Sharing

Buyer data is shared ONLY with Yamato Transport Co., Ltd. for delivery. We do not sell or share data with any other third parties.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Customer personal data is retained for 12 months then permanently deleted. Order IDs and tracking numbers may be retained up to 7 years for tax purposes per Japanese law.

6. Access Management

Access to SP-API credentials and customer PII is restricted to a single authorized individual (sole operator). Access reviewed monthly. Remote access requires VPN and SSH key authentication.

7. Incident Response

In the event of a security incident: Amazon will be notified at security@amazon.com within 24 hours. SP-API access will be suspended immediately. Affected customers notified per applicable law.
